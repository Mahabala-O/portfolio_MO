# <span>Mikhail Orlov</span>

Before starting my MSBA at UC Irvine's Merage School of Business, I spent three years in operations and data roles across healthcare and e-commerce. At Accendra Health I maintained the Power BI reporting behind our Southern California operations; at a DTC startup I built the SQL reporting the team used to find where things were breaking.

My degree is in Human-Computer Interaction, so I start with who will use the analysis and what they need to decide, then work backward to the data.

At UCI I'm studying statistical modeling, machine learning, forecasting, and optimization. I graduate in 2027 and I'm looking for business operations and strategy analyst roles.
