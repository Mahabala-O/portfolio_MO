## About

Before starting my MSBA at UC Irvine's Merage School of Business, I spent three years in operations and data roles across healthcare and e-commerce. My background in Human-Computer Interaction shapes how I work with data — I think about the person behind the numbers, which changes how I frame problems and communicate findings.
