# Mikhail Orlov 

Before starting my MSBA at UC Irvine's Merage School of Business, I spent three years in operations and data roles across healthcare and e-commerce. At Accendra Health I maintained the Power BI reporting behind our Southern California operations; at a DTC startup I built the SQL reporting the team used to find where things were breaking.
My background in Human-Computer Interaction shapes how I work with data — I think about the person behind the numbers, which changes how I frame problems and communicate findings.
At UCI I'm focused on statistical modeling, machine learning, and supply chain analytics, heading toward operations and business analytics roles. Open to connecting.
