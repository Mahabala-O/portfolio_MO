## About

Before starting my MSBA at UC Irvine's Merage School of Business, I spent three years in operations and data roles in healthcare and e-commerce.

**Accendra Health** (Operations Specialist, 2025–2026). I analyzed operational workflows across **10 Southern California branch locations** to find process bottlenecks and service gaps, which shaped logistics and staffing decisions. I maintained the **weekly Power BI dashboards** leadership used to track sales volume and fulfillment KPIs, and traced recurring account and system issues to their root cause so engineering could fix them.

**Calmarsi** (Customer Success & Operations, DTC e-commerce, 2022–2024). I built **weekly SQL reporting** on repeat contacts and fulfillment exceptions, and standardized metric definitions and data validation so the reporting stayed accurate as data volume grew.

My background in Human-Computer Interaction shapes how I work with data — I think about the person behind the numbers, which changes how I frame problems and communicate findings.

At UCI I'm focused on statistical modeling, machine learning, and supply chain analytics, heading toward operations and business analytics roles. Open to connecting.
